ABOUT TRANSFERTRACKER

Football Transfers,
Tracked Simply.

TransferTracker is a football transfer management platform designed to make player movements, transfer values and club changes easy to explore and manage.

THE PROJECT

What is TransferTracker?

TransferTracker allows users to explore football transfer records, search for players, filter transfers by club and position, and view detailed player information.

The platform also includes an administrative dashboard where authorized administrators can add, edit and delete transfer records. The data is stored in MongoDB and accessed through a Node.js and Express backend.

FEATURES

What You Can Do

🔎 Explore Transfers

Search and filter transfer records by player, club and position.

⚽ Club Profiles

Explore clubs and see their incoming and outgoing transfers.

📊 Transfer Data

View transfer values, player positions, dates and transfer status.

🔐 Admin Dashboard

Authorized administrators can manage transfer records through a protected dashboard.

PROJECT GOAL

Built to Learn. Built to Grow.

TransferTracker was built as a full-stack learning project focused on combining frontend development, backend APIs, database management and authentication into one practical application.
